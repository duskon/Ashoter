Ashoter – Space Typing Arcade Shooter Game by dushi_dev

Play Ashoter online, a fast-paced 3D space typing arcade shooter game inspired by ZType and developed by dushi_dev (dush_dev). Defend your stealth fighter against waves of incoming enemy words by typing accurately and rapidly.

Key Features

  • Progressive Enemy Waves: Battle through Wave 001, Wave 002, and beyond with carrier ships, fragments, and boss encounters.
  • Load Your Own Text: Practice typing with custom passages, code snippets, or study notes.
  • Real-Time Typing Stats: Track final score, wave reached, typing accuracy percentage, and longest streak.
  • Customizable Settings: Adjust synthesized Web Audio laser sound effects, ambient space music, and word font sizes.
